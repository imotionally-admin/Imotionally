Sara and the Little Fairy

Page 1

Sara loved fairy stories.

Every night, she looked out of her window and wished,

“Maybe a fairy will visit me someday.”

One morning, Sara found something tiny shining in the grass.

It was a little fairy!

Page 2

The fairy had tiny wings and a sparkly pink dress.

But she looked sad.

“My magic is gone,” she whispered.

Sara gently asked,

“Can I help you?”

The fairy nodded.

“Yes! I need to find my lost sparkle.”

Page 3

Sara and the fairy searched the garden.

They looked under the big green leaves.

They looked behind the flowers.

They even peeked inside a little mushroom!

But there was no sparkle.

Then Sara heard a tiny sound.

“Chirp, chirp!”

A little bird was stuck in a bush.

Page 4

Sara carefully moved the branches.

“Don’t worry, little bird,” she said.

The bird flew away happily.

The fairy smiled.

Suddenly…

TWINKLE!

A tiny golden sparkle appeared on her wing.

“Look!” cried Sara.

“My magic is coming back!”

Page 5

They kept walking.

Soon they found a little bunny sitting all alone.

Sara shared her carrot with the bunny.

The bunny hopped happily away.

TWINKLE!

Another golden sparkle appeared.

The fairy's wings began to glow.

Page 6

The fairy looked at Sara with a big smile.

“I know where my magic was hiding!”

“Where?” Sara asked.

“In your kind heart!”

The fairy waved her little wand.

POOF!

The whole garden filled with tiny glowing stars.

Page 7

That evening, Sara sat by her window.

The little fairy flew up into the moonlight.

“Will you come back?” Sara asked.

“Of course!” said the fairy.

“Magic always comes back to a kind heart.”

Sara smiled.


Page 8

And from that night on, she always looked for a little sparkle in her garden.

The End.
